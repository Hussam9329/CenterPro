23. FIXED SALARY FULL-MONTH LOGIC
For an employee employed for the full payroll month:
Base Salary = Fixed Salary
regardless of how many attendance days exceed the normal work amount.
Absences and other financial adjustments are applied AFTER the fixed Base Salary.
Example:
Fixed Salary:
1,000,000

1 excused absence:
- Daily Rate

Bonus:
+50,000

Other deduction:
-25,000
________________________________________
24. FIXED SALARY PARTIAL FIRST/LAST MONTH
If a Fixed Salary employee starts during the middle of the month or their employment ends during the middle of the month:
Do NOT automatically give the complete Fixed Salary.
For that partial employment month:
Partial Base Salary =
Attendance Days × Effective Daily Rate
Cap this value at:
Fixed Salary
Then apply absence deductions, other deductions, and bonuses normally.
Example:
Started during the month
Attendance = 8 days
Daily Rate = 25,000

Partial Base =
8 × 25,000
= 200,000
This special partial-month logic applies only to the first or final partial employment month.
